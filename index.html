About Samuel

I'm a Level 3 qualified personal trainer based at PureGym London Stratford, specialising in strength training alongside mobility and flexibility development.

I help clients build strong, capable bodies while improving movement quality, reducing injury risk and raising overall performance. My coaching comes down to three things: proper technique, balanced programming and long-term progress — not punishing sessions you dread and quit after a month.

By pairing strength work with mobility and flexibility training, my clients don't just get stronger — they move better and feel more confident in their own bodies. Whether you've never touched a barbell or you've been training for years, there's a version of this that fits you.

Common Questions

Where are you based?

I train clients at PureGym London Stratford, 68/7 The Mall, Stratford Centre, London E15 1XQ. The gym is inside the Stratford Centre — about two minutes from Stratford station and directly opposite Westfield Stratford City.

How much does a personal trainer in Stratford cost?

My packages are monthly: £160 for one session a week, £296 for two, £420 for three and £528 for four. That works out at £40 down to £33 per session — the more consistently you train, the lower the rate.

Is the first session really free?

Yes — a free taster session, no cost and no obligation. You get a proper session, we talk through your goals, and you decide afterwards whether you want to carry on. No hard sell.

Do I need a PureGym membership?

Yes — sessions happen on the PureGym Stratford gym floor, so you'll need a membership to get in. Stratford memberships start from around £23.99 a month and the gym is open 24/7.

I've never trained before — is that a problem?

Not at all — beginners are one of my specialisms. The Beginner Conditioning package exists for exactly this: technique, movement foundations and confidence first, before we add any serious load.

Which areas do you cover?

Clients come to Stratford from across Newham and East London — Maryland, West Ham, Bow, Leyton, Leytonstone, Hackney Wick, Forest Gate, Plaistow, Canning Town and Mile End, plus Olympic Park and East Village residents.

What qualifications do you hold?

Level 3 Personal Trainer and Level 2 Gym Instructor, with specialisms in strength training, body fat reduction, mobility and flexibility, and training special populations.
